The game

Read every turn. Plan every mission.

Lead a squad of metahumans: explore a board that unfolds as you advance, combine your agents' powers on the combat timeline and run The Dawn's headquarters between missions.

Combat on a timeline

Follow the combat timeline, manage your agents' energy and play their skill cards. Strike, protect or support: every decision shapes the balance of your squad.

A board that unfolds

Roll the die to gain movement points, study the available paths and choose your destination. The fog hides what comes next: push forward to uncover encounters, battles and objectives.

Build your squad

Recruit agents with different abilities, assemble your team and look for synergies between its members. Prepare their skills and equipment to shape your strategy.

Headquarters, your base

Between missions, manage your team, review objectives, craft equipment and organize expeditions. Preparation is part of the adventure.

A campaign that starts in New York

Choose your mission on the map, follow the objectives of each zone and unlock the next ones as the campaign advances.

Mini-games

Rhythm, infiltration, engineering and survival challenges break up the missions and reward the squad.
