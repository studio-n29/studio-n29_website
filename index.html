About

I like mechanics that feel good in the hands: readable combat, a spell that responds at the right moment, networking you never notice. My experience also gave me full-stack web, LLM / AI and prompt-engineering skills.

I fit into a team quickly, communicate clearly and like shipping code that others can pick up.
