1. Who we are

Heroes Dawn is a game published by Studio N29, operated by Djason Nathiez, an independent game developer based in France (n29-studio.fr). Studio N29 is the “data controller” for the personal data described here (“we”, “us”).

Contact for any privacy question or request: dm.nathiez@gmail.com.

This policy applies to the Heroes Dawn game and its online services. It does not cover the app stores or other third-party services you use to download or pay for the game (Google Play, Apple App Store, Steam…), which have their own policies.

3. Data we do not collect

  • Your precise or approximate location, contacts, photos, files, calendar, camera or microphone.
  • Your real name, postal address, phone number, or payment card details.
  • Advertising identifiers for advertising purposes: Heroes Dawn shows no ads and shares no data with advertisers.
  • Health, financial, biometric or other sensitive data.

4. Why we use your data

We use your data only to run and improve the game: to provide accounts, cloud saves, tournaments and purchases; to keep the service secure and fix problems; to answer your requests; and, with your consent, to measure how the game is played. We do not sell your personal data, we do not “share” it for cross-context behavioural advertising, and we do not take automated decisions with legal effects on you.

Where the GDPR or similar laws apply, our legal bases are:

  • Performance of a contract: account, cloud save, tournaments and purchases.
  • Consent: anonymous usage statistics. Refusing has no effect on gameplay. You can withdraw consent at any time in Settings ▸ Account and privacy, without affecting what was done before.
  • Legitimate interests: security, fraud and abuse prevention, and keeping the service working.
  • Legal obligation: keeping purchase records as required by accounting and tax rules.

6. International transfers

Heroes Dawn is available worldwide. Our account and tournament data is hosted in the European Union. Some providers (for example Unity, Google, Apple) may process data in other countries, including the United States. When personal data leaves the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, we rely on safeguards recognised by law, such as the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses or an adequacy decision, as put in place by those providers.

7. How long we keep it

  • Account and online save: for as long as your account exists. Accounts inactive for 36 months may be deleted after notice by e-mail where possible.
  • Tournament data: team snapshots are not used for duels after 60 days. Scores are kept for the history of past editions until your account is deleted.
  • Usage statistics: kept by the analytics provider for a limited period, then deleted or aggregated. Statistics are not linked to your e-mail address.
  • Purchase records: for the period required by accounting and tax law, and by the store.
  • Server logs: kept for a short period for security and debugging.
  • Local data: stays on your device until you erase it or uninstall the game.

8. Your choices and rights

Choices in the game

  • Play without an account.
  • Turn anonymous statistics on or off at any time in Settings ▸ Account and privacy.
  • Erase your progress with Settings ▸ Account and privacy ▸ Erase save (this also erases the online save of your account).

European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland

You have the right to access your data, have it corrected or deleted, restrict or object to its processing, receive it in a portable format, and withdraw consent at any time. You may also lodge a complaint with your data protection authority (in France: the CNIL, cnil.fr).

United States (California and other states)

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to know what personal data we hold, to access, correct and delete it, and not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights. We do not sell personal data and do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising, so there is nothing to opt out of. We do not knowingly process sensitive personal data.

Brazil, Canada, Australia, Japan, South Korea and elsewhere

Where your local law gives you rights such as access, correction, deletion, portability or objection, we honour them. Tell us what you need and we will handle it in line with the law that applies to you.

How to exercise your rights

Write to dm.nathiez@gmail.com, preferably from the e-mail address of your account. We may ask you to confirm your identity. We answer within 30 days. Requests are free unless they are manifestly excessive.

9. Deleting your data

You can ask us to delete your account and all associated data at any time, without having to reinstall or reopen the game, by following the instructions on the account and data deletion page. Deletion is completed within 30 days. Data we must keep by law (such as purchase records) is kept only for the required period.

10. Children

Heroes Dawn is not directed at children under 13, or under the minimum age set by the law of your country if it is higher (for example up to 16 in some EU countries) and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has given us personal data, write to us and we will delete it. Parents and guardians of players who are minors may exercise the rights above on their behalf.

11. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/WSS). On our servers, each player can only access their own account data (row-level security). Passwords are handled by our authentication provider and are never readable by us. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affecting your rights occurs, we will notify you and the authorities as the law requires.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when we add a feature. The date at the top shows the latest version, and we will announce important changes in the game or on our website before they take effect. Where the law requires it, we will ask for your consent again.
