Heroes Dawn · Studio N29

Delete your account and data

You do not need to reinstall or open the game to make this request.

Request deletion of your account

  1. Send an e-mail to dm.nathiez@gmail.com with the subject “Heroes Dawn – Account deletion request”.
  2. Send it from the e-mail address of your account (or the address used by your Google or Apple sign-in). This is how we confirm the account is yours. Adding your player tag helps but is optional.
  3. We confirm by reply and complete the deletion within 30 days.

Open a pre-filled e-mail

What is deleted

  • Your account (e-mail address, account identifier, linked Google or Apple identifier).
  • Your online save: progress, team, virtual currencies, settings, nickname and player tag.
  • Your tournament data: team, points and ranking.
  • Your anonymous usage statistics, where they can be linked to your account. They are not linked to your e-mail address.

Virtual items and currencies tied to the account are permanently lost and cannot be restored, including items you bought.

What may be kept, and for how long

  • Purchase records (product and transaction identifiers), only for the period required by accounting and tax law. Payment records held by Google Play, the Apple App Store or Steam are governed by those stores.
  • Security logs, for a short period, to prevent fraud and abuse.

Delete only part of your data

  • Stop usage statistics: in the game, Settings ▸ Account and privacy, turn off “Share anonymous statistics”.
  • Erase your save but keep your account: Settings ▸ Account and privacy ▸ Erase save.
  • Local data on your device is removed when you uninstall the game.

See our Privacy Policy for details on how your data is used.
