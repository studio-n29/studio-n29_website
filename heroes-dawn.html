The game

A squad. A world that has changed. One choice at a time.

Five years after the Paris Supernova, humans have become metahumans: powers awaken and the world is torn between fear, fascination and hope. Join The Dawn, an organization protecting metahumans and civilians in the Apex Universe, prepare your squad and use your agents' powers to overcome every obstacle.

Every mission begins with a choice: pick your route across the board, recruit the right agents and prepare your next outing at headquarters. Heroes Dawn is out on January 15, 2027 on Android, iOS and Steam.

The world

The world, in brief.

Heroes Dawn is set in the Apex Universe, the shared universe of STUDIO N29's games. Here is what you need to know before joining The Dawn.

  1. 1981

    The Apex Unit

    In the middle of the Cold War, the United States creates the Apex Unit: the very first metahumans.

  2. 2026

    The Paris Supernova

    One night, a phenomenon strikes Paris. Across the world, people's genes are altered. For most, nothing changes; in some, a power lies dormant.

  3. 5 years later

    Heroes Dawn

    Powers awaken after a sudden shock. The world is torn between fear, fascination and hope, and institutions try to control the metahumans. You join The Dawn.

The Dawn

An organization founded by metahumans who chose not to hide. Its symbol is a golden four-pointed star. It protects metahumans and civilians.

The AMIF

An agency that claims to supervise metahumans for their safety, but studies and takes their abilities. The Dawn's main official threat.

The Apex

The everyday nickname of metahumans, taken from the original Apex Unit. The official term remains “metahuman”.

Gameplay

How it plays.

Combat on a timeline

Follow the combat timeline, manage your agents' energy and play their skill cards. Strike, protect or support: every decision shapes the balance of your squad.

A board that unfolds

Roll the die to gain movement points, study the available paths and choose your destination. The fog hides what comes next: push forward to uncover encounters, battles and objectives.

Build your squad

Recruit agents with different abilities, assemble your team and look for synergies between its members. Prepare their skills and equipment to shape your strategy.

Headquarters, your base

Between missions, manage your team, review objectives, craft equipment and organize expeditions. Preparation is part of the adventure.

A campaign that starts in New York

Choose your mission on the map, follow the objectives of each zone and unlock the next ones as the campaign advances.

Mini-games

Rhythm, infiltration, engineering and survival challenges break up the missions and reward the squad.

Under the hood

Built to last.

Heroes Dawn is built in Unity 6 with a layered clean architecture: an MVP interface, a pure-C# domain package, dependency injection and EditMode / PlayMode tests. In-editor tools let the team author items, skills, agents and narration, and balance the game from spreadsheets.

  • Unity 6
  • C#
  • MVP
  • VContainer
  • MessagePipe
  • UniTask
  • EditMode / PlayMode tests
  • Editor tools
  • Supabase-ready
