The project

Heroes Dawn is a 3D fantasy RPG designed for mobile, set in the Apex Universe, the shared universe of Studio N29's projects. It combines turn-based combat on an active timeline with HQ management and mechanics inspired by life sims and MMORPGs.

My contribution

  • Turn-based combat with an active timeline that makes the action order readable and strategic.
  • HQ management: rooms, expeditions, recruitment and squad progression, designed for mobile.
  • Production documentation: GDD, game-design specs, art direction, UI flows and roadmap laid out before writing code.
  • Coherent universe: the Apex Universe, shared across Studio N29 projects.
