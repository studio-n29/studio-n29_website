The project

Heroes Dawn is a mobile-first management RPG set in the Apex Universe, the shared universe of Studio N29's projects. A squad of meta-humans explores New York zone by zone: rolling dice across a board, fighting turn-based battles on an active timeline and managing their HQ between missions. The current build (v0.9.0) offers a playable Brooklyn campaign in four zones.

My contribution

  • Turn-based combat on an active timeline, with team synergy replacing the former combo system.
  • Playable Brooklyn campaign: four zones (Red Hook, Brownsville, Coney Island, Dumbo and the bridge) with objectives, enemies and narration.
  • Onboarding and progression: first-login flow (cinematic, narrative nickname, campaign), HQ quest tracking (main and side quests) and an account architecture ready for Supabase.
  • Clean architecture: layered code with an MVP interface, a pure-C# domain package, VContainer, MessagePipe, UniTask and EditMode / PlayMode tests.
  • In-editor design tools (Unity windows) for items, skills, agents and narration, CSV export for design spreadsheets, and balancing tools (spreadsheet import and simulator).
  • Production documentation: GDD, game-design specs, art direction, UI flows and a roadmap (Steam Early Access, then mobile) laid out before writing code.
