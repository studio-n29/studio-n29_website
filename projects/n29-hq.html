The project

N29 HQ is the internal app I built to run STUDIO N29 day to day. It is a local-first, installable web app (PWA) that works offline and on my phone, with the same look as this site. Screenshots use fictional demo data.

My contribution

  • Projects and tasks: several projects, each with its own kanban board and Markdown documents edited visually (fonts, sizes, styles, tables).
  • Time tracking: timer and calendar entries with date-range reports, in the spirit of Toggl.
  • Quotes and invoices following French legal requirements: continuous numbering, hash-chained issued documents, credit notes, deposits.
  • End-to-end encrypted sync: AES-GCM with a passphrase, stored on Supabase with row-level security, 3-way merge across devices.
  • Production tools: an improved Apex prompt generator for illustration work.
