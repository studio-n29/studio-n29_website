The project

In Beat Strike the level scrolls, enemies show up and the player chains tempo-synced interactions to attack and dodge. I developed the rhythm engine, the main gameplay loop and a Unity tool (Capacity Tool) to compose patterns in real time.

My contribution

  • High-precision rhythm engine, designed to stay accurate on mobile.
  • Capacity Tool: a Unity editor with a timeline (BPM, duration, interaction type: attack, up / down dodge…) that designers can use without touching code.
  • Patterns per BPM: a library of attack and defence patterns, picked according to the level's tempo.
  • Gameplay loop: level chunk sequencing, attack and defence phases, interaction spawning and combat triggering.
  • Performance: object pooling for interactions to avoid allocations during play.
