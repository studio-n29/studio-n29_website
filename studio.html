The studio

Games designed around the player's choices.

STUDIO N29 is an independent studio. We start from the experience we want players to live, then shape every system, every screen and every world around it.

A good game gives you decisions that matter, in a world you want to stay in.

Design philosophy

How we design.

01

Choice at the centre

Every mission begins with a choice. We design decisions the player can read, weigh and own, not just reflexes to react to.

02

Readable above all

Players should always understand what is happening and why. Clear information and a clean interface come before spectacle.

03

Feel first

A mechanic is finished when it feels right in the hands, not merely when it works. We iterate until the sensation is there.

04

Characters that matter

Heroes are more than silhouettes: each one has their own powers and their own story.

05

Worlds that echo

Our games share one universe, the Apex Universe. Characters, factions and places travel from one game to the next.

06

Focused rather than long

We prefer a concentrated adventure with detours that matter over padding. Every hour must earn its place.
